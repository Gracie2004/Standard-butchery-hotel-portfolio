ABOUT US

Welcome to Standard Butchery & Hotel

Standard Butchery & Hotel is a trusted food destination located along the Eldoret–Kitale Road in Soy. We bring together a hotel and a butchery under one roof, offering our customers delicious meals and fresh, quality meat.

We are committed to providing quality food, fresh meat, excellent service and value for money. Whether you are looking for a satisfying meal or freshly prepared meat, we are ready to serve you.
