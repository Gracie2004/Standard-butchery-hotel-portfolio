ABOUT US

Welcome to Standard Butchery & Hotel

Standard Butchery & Hotel is a food destination located along Eldoret–Kitale Road in Soy. We bring together a hotel and a butchery under one roof, offering delicious meals and fresh, quality meat.

We are committed to providing quality food, fresh meat, excellent service and value for money. Whether you need a satisfying meal or freshly prepared meat, we are ready to serve you.
